About Pillar-Star

Turning Africa’s resources into world-class products

A Nigerian, multi-sector agro-industrial enterprise with one enduring mandate: to serve families, industry and the continent’s economic development.

Executive overview

Fifteen years of building from the ground up

Pillar-Star Industries Limited is a Nigerian-incorporated, multi-sector agro-industrial enterprise. Our mandate is to transform West Africa’s abundant agricultural and mineral resources into world-class products that serve families, industry and the continent’s broader economic development agenda.

The company was established on 2nd March 2009 and is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission under RC 804323. We are headquartered in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, a commercially strategic hub at the heart of Nigeria’s South-South economy.

Over more than fifteen years of continuous operation, Pillar-Star has built the institutional relationships, regulatory familiarity and technical capability required to execute complex, capital-intensive agro-industrial projects at scale.

Three divisions are active today: agricultural production, agro-processing, and civil engineering and infrastructure. Chemical manufacturing is planned as the next stage of our growth. This integration from farmgate to finished product gives us control of supply, cost efficiency and resilience against commodity price volatility and foreign exchange fluctuation.

Vision

Contribute to Africa’s food security by empowering youths and women, creating safer communities, and driving sustainable industrialisation.

Mission

Produce healthy, high-quality, competitively priced products through agriculture, agro-processing and chemical manufacturing, serving families, communities and cooperative partners with honesty and compassion.
